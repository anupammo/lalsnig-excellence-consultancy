About us

Business excellence consultants with plant-floor experience

We build operational excellence through lean manufacturing, Lean Six Sigma and workforce capability — turning waste, variation and process complexity into measurable business performance.

Our work starts where the loss actually is, not where the org chart says it should be. Value stream mapping, time study, root cause analysis and shop-floor data give us a repeatable way to find it; Kaizen, standard work and structured training make sure the gain is still there a year later.

Underneath the improvement method sits deep engineering specialism. Eighteen years inside conveyor and bulk material handling means we can go from a plant-wide productivity question down to a belt splice failure without changing consultants — and that is what separates a diagnosis from a recommendation.

Frequently asked

Business excellence questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? A short call is usually faster than a long email.

A business excellence consultant finds where an operation loses output, cost or quality, fixes the cause rather than the symptom, and leaves the client able to keep doing it. In practice that means mapping the value stream, measuring the work as it is really performed, running structured root cause analysis on the losses that matter, redesigning the process or the maintenance regime around the findings, and training the people who run it so the gain holds.

We work on the shop floor, not in the boardroom, and every recommendation is costed and measurable. Our lead consultant spent 18+ years inside manufacturing and application engineering, so the analysis goes all the way down to the equipment when it needs to: a plant-wide productivity question and a belt splice failure can be answered by the same person, in the same engagement.
