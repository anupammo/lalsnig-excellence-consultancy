01 · Logo

Logo & clear space

Primary — light backgrounds

Horizontal lockup for headers and footers — on navy, always on a white chip, never knocked out

App icon

Do

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the “L” on all four sides.
  • Minimum width 120 px on screen / 30 mm in print.
  • Use the white chip whenever the background is darker than --ls-grey-100.

Don’t

  • Recolour, outline, rotate or add effects to the mark.
  • Place the transparent logo directly on a photograph.
  • Re-typeset the wordmark — it is artwork, not live text.

Open action: the current logo files are raster extractions from the company deck. Request vector originals (AI/EPS/SVG) from the client before Phase 2 so the mark stays crisp at every size. Tracked in docs/03-progress-checklist.md.

The one rule that keeps this palette accessible

Gold and orange are dark-background colours. On navy they clear AA comfortably. On white, #C5912B hits only 2.8:1 and #E46C0A only 3.3:1 — so on light surfaces use --ls-gold-700 for gold text, --ls-orange-700 for CTA fills, and keep #E46C0A for rules, icons and shapes where contrast is decorative.

06 · Quality bar

What this foundation guarantees

WCAG 2.1 AA

Every text/background pair in the token set is measured and documented. Skip link, visible focus ring, landmark regions and one H1 per page.

Self-hosted assets

Bootstrap and every webfont are served from this origin — no CDN, no Google Fonts call. Google Analytics 4 is the one third-party script, added on the client’s instruction; it sets cookies and needs a privacy notice.

Layout-stable

Every image ships explicit width/height or an aspect-ratio box, so CLS stays at zero while photos stream in.

Reduced motion

Reveal animations, smooth scrolling and hover lifts all switch off under prefers-reduced-motion.
